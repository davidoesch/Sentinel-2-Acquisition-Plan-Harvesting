Method and caveats

For each STAC item, latency = properties.created − properties.datetime. Footprints are split into H3 resolution 7 sample cells. Each sample cell keeps the scene that published it first on that acquisition date. The map shows the median over the sample cells of each resolution 5 hexagon. Hexagons are only stored on dates with an acquisition.

Items published more than 240 h after acquisition are treated as reprocessing and excluded from the map (they are still listed with a "reprocessed" tag). The value measures when a scene became visible in this catalog, not ESA's own publication time. Metadata only: no imagery is downloaded and no cloud masking is applied.
